Expiry: Renewal Radar

Passports, visas, warranties

What Expiry collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Expiry uses the camera when you choose "Scan a document", to read a date off a page or receipt. The page is read on the phone, never uploaded, and the text it contains is dropped once the date is found. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
