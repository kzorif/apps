Din: Who Said It?

Record friends. Play the game.

What Din collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

The microphone records only when you press record. Recordings are stored on your iPhone and shared only when you choose to send a pack, for example by AirDrop. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
