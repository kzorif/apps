Low Gear: Run-Walk Coach

Walk and run by heart rate

What Low Gear collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

your heart rate on your Apple Watch during a run, and saves each session to Health as a running workout None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
