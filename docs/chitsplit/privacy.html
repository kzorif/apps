ChitSplit

Photo the bill, split it fair

What ChitSplit collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

The camera or a photo you pick is read on your device by Apple's text recognition to find the items on the bill. Photos are not uploaded or kept after the split. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
