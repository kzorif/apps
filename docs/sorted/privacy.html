Sorted: Screenshot Cleaner

Search and clear screenshots

What Sorted collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Sorted reads the screenshots in your photo library on your phone: it recognises the text in them and sorts them into piles. It deletes only the ones you confirm, and they go to Recently Deleted in Photos for 30 days. The text and the sorting stay on the device; no photo or text is sent anywhere. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
