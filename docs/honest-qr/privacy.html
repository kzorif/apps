honest qr · privacy

Honest QR: Static Code Maker

No server. No expiry.

What Honest QR collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

The camera is used only to read QR codes and barcodes you point it at, on your device. Photos you choose to scan or use as a logo are read on your device and are never uploaded. Honest QR makes no network requests. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
