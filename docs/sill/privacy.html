Sill: Plant Light Meter

Measure the spot, not the room

What Sill collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Sill uses the camera to read the brightness where your plant lives. No photo is taken, stored or sent; only the camera's exposure settings are read, on the device. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
