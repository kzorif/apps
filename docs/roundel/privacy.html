Roundel: HIIT & EMOM Timer

Tabata AMRAP Interval Circuits

What Roundel collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

your heart rate and energy during a workout (only if you allow it) on your Apple Watch, to show them with the workout in Health, and starts a workout session so cues keep firing with your wrist down None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
