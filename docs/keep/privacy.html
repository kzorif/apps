Privacy Policy

Keep: Castles of the World — a castles atlas and daily reveal game.

What Keep: Castles of the World collects

Nothing. Keep: Castles of the World has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and never sends your data anywhere. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.

Apple services

Where the app shows maps or street-level imagery, those are loaded by Apple Maps under Apple's privacy policy. Siri and Shortcuts, where offered, run on your device.
