Highpoint: Bouldering Journal

Why you fall, what to train

What Highpoint collects

Nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you put in the app stays on your iPhone: your projects and attempts, the clips you attach, your baseline tests, workouts, guides you have read, your training plan and your settings. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

Clips

You choose a video with Apple's photo picker, so Highpoint never asks for access to your photo library. The app keeps a compressed copy in its own storage; your original is not touched. You can delete any clip, or everything, in Settings.

The guides

The animated guides play from files inside the app. They need no internet connection and load nothing from a server.

Reminders

If you turn on the optional re-test reminder, the app schedules one local notification on your iPhone. It is not sent through any server. You can turn it off in Settings.

Your data is yours

Settings → Export everything writes a folder with JSON and CSV files and your clips, free, whenever you want. Settings → Delete everything removes it all from the phone.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
