Chapters: Your Week, a Story

Private journal, on-device AI

What Chapters collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Chapters uses the microphone and on-device speech recognition only while you hold the Hold to speak button, to turn your voice into text. Audio is never saved or sent anywhere, and speech is converted on the iPhone itself. Notifications are optional local reminders. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
