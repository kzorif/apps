Tooth Fairy Countdown

Never miss the tooth fairy

What Tooth Fairy Countdown collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Optionally, a photo you choose from your library to keep with a tooth. It is picked with Apple's photo picker, so the app never sees the rest of your library, and the photo stays on your phone. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
