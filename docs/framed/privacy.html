Framed: Batch Watermark

Presets, sizes, full quality

What Framed collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Photos you choose are read on your device only, to apply your watermark and size. Framed saves finished photos to your library with add-only access and never reads your library, your location or your other photos. Framed makes no network requests. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
