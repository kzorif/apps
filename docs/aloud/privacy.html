Aloud: PDF to Audio Files

Private voices, chapters, M4A

What Aloud collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere. It stays on your device.

What it uses on your iPhone

Aloud reads only the documents you choose and the one web link you paste, on your device. It saves audio files in its own storage, which also shows in Files. It uses no camera, microphone, location or contacts, and has no accounts or analytics. None of it is ever sent to us, and you can turn access off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no personal information about you from a purchase.
